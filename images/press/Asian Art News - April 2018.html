Coming Together Again

April 18, 2018
Asian Art News — April 2018. Review of Lydia Janssen's work at REDSEA Gallery, Singapore.
"Lydia Janssen's abstract expressionist paintings are dynamic, intimate narratives of disaster and renewal."

Her achievement is not one easily obtained: it is hard-won — emotionally, physically, and intellectually. She speaks to passion and to the sensuality of life, as well as to the liberation of the damaged spirit and its rebuilding.

Janssen's work occupies a rare territory in contemporary painting — one where the body's memory, desire for movement, and the raw energy of abstract expressionism converge on linen canvases of unusual scale and intimacy. Working in oil, charcoal and pastel, she constructs layered surfaces that bear witness to physical and emotional experience with an almost archaeological density.
