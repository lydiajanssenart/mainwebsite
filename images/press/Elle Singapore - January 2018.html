Elle Culture: The Exhibition to Catch

January 20, 2018
Elle Singapore, Living section — January 2018

Elle Singapore's Living section named Lydia Janssen's debut Singapore solo exhibition, All the King's Horses at REDSEA Gallery, as the exhibition to catch in January 2018.

The exhibition, opening 27 January at REDSEA Gallery, Dempsey Hill, brought together a new body of autobiographical paintings — large-scale works in oil, charcoal and pastel on linen — charting Janssen's journey from professional dancer to painter over the previous five years.
